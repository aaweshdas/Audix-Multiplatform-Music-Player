How do you share >90% of your codebase across Android and Windows/macOS/Linux without sacrificing 60fps performance or native audio hardware control?

Over the past few months, I architected and shipped Nightly 🌙 — an open-source media and cinema client built with Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) and Compose Multiplatform.

Here is an architectural breakdown of how the system works under the hood 🧵👇

1️⃣ UNIFIED UI & PRESENTATION (Compose Multiplatform + Material 3)
Instead of maintaining separate Swift and Jetpack Compose/WPF codebases, the entire UI is written in declarative Compose Multiplatform.
- Adaptive Breakpoints: The layout dynamically reconfigures between mobile phone portrait views, tablet split-panes, and wide desktop multi-column stations.
- Custom Shaders & Glassmorphism: Frosted background blurs, ambient glow backdrops, and hardware-accelerated fluid transitions.

2️⃣ THE AUDIO ENGINE: DUAL NATIVE RUNTIMES
Audio is latency-sensitive. A single abstraction doesn’t cut it across operating systems.
- Android Runtime: Built on AndroidX Media3 (ExoPlayer), with foreground playback services, lock-screen MediaSession notifications, audio focus handling, and automatic ducking.
- Desktop Runtime: Native libVLC C-bindings via VLCJ, granting low-latency PCM stream extraction, gapless track transitions, and native cross-platform audio driver access.

3️⃣ 28-BAND AUDIOPHILE FFT VISUALIZER
Rather than generating fake random bouncing bars, Nightly implements a mathematical 512-point Cooley-Tukey Radix-2 FFT algorithm:
- Raw audio byte chunks are captured and transformed into frequency domain spectra in real-time.
- Hann windowing is applied to eliminate spectral leakage.
- Energy is binned across 28 logarithmic frequency bands tuned to human auditory perception (from 20 Hz sub-bass to 20 kHz treble).

4️⃣ DECOUPLED EXTENSION SPI ARCHITECTURE
To aggregate catalogs without tight coupling, I designed a Service Provider Interface (SPI):
- Audio & Metadata Providers (Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, SoundCloud, RadioBrowser) operate as modular extensions.
- Dynamic Fallback: If a track source is unavailable or geo-blocked, the state machine automatically negotiates mirrors without interrupting the playback queue.
- Type-Safe Offline Caching: Driven by SQLDelight with optimized SQLite schemas for offline favorites, listening history, and local audio indexing.

5️⃣ IN-APP CINEMA & SECURITY SHIELD
Streaming web content in an application poses major security risks (untrusted scripts, rogue redirects, pop-ups).
- We implemented a sandboxed WebView container with custom script injection that blocks popups, ad telemetry, and iframe hijacking.
- An intelligent resolver talks to Cinemeta APIs to map title queries to IMDb IDs (`tt...`) and resolve clean multi-server video streams.

6️⃣ AI-POWERED SPEECH-TO-TEXT SEARCH
Voice search shouldn’t require proprietary voice assistants. Nightly integrates:
- Dual-engine STT using Whisper Large V3 Turbo (via Groq ultra-low-latency API) and Deepgram Nova-2.
- Real-time Voice Activity Detection (VAD) with instant query transcription for songs, artists, and movies.

Tech Stack Summary:
• Language: Kotlin 2.x
• Framework: Compose Multiplatform (Desktop + Android)
• Database: SQLDelight (SQLite)
• Networking: Ktor Client with Coroutines & Flow
• Audio/Video: AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer + VLCJ / libVLC
• AI / STT: Whisper Large V3 Turbo & Deepgram Nova-2
