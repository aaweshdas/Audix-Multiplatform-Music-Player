🚀 Excited to officially introduce Nightly 🌙 — a modern, privacy-first media player that merges all your favorite audio and cinema into one unified cross-platform app.

No ads. No tracking. No paywalled background play. Just pure media freedom.

Built from scratch with Kotlin Multiplatform & Compose, Nightly works seamlessly across both Android and Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux).

Here’s what’s inside:

🎧 Universal Audio Hub
Stream and search seamlessly across Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, SoundCloud, and global radio stations without jumping between apps.

🎬 In-App Cinema Theater
Watch movies and series right inside the application with smart IMDb resolution, multi-server stream mirrors, and zero intrusive pop-ups.

📊 Real-Time 28-Band FFT Visualizer
Audiophile-grade audio visualization computed directly from raw PCM audio using mathematical Cooley-Tukey FFT.

🎙️ AI-Powered Voice Search
Search any song, artist, or movie hands-free using dual Whisper Large V3 Turbo and Deepgram Nova-2 speech-to-text models.

🎨 Stunning Modern UI
Fluid glassmorphic styling, dynamic Material 3 themes, synced synchronized lyrics, and lock-screen background controls.

Best of all? It’s 100% Free & Open-Source on GitHub! 🌟
