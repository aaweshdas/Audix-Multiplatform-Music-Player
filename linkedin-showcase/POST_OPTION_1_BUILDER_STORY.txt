Tired of ad interruptions, paywalled background play, and juggling separate apps for music and video?

I decided to stop complaining and built something better.

Meet Nightly 🌙 — a modern, privacy-focused, cross-platform audio & cinema streaming powerhouse built completely with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose.

Here is why I built it from the ground up:

Most modern streaming applications are bloated. They consume gigabytes of RAM, force telemetry on users, block basic features like background playback behind subscriptions, and lock you into a single catalog.

I wanted an all-in-one media workstation that is:
✅ 100% Free & Open-Source (Zero ads, zero tracking)
✅ Unified across Android and Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux)
✅ Aggregated across Spotify, YouTube Music, JioSaavn, SoundCloud, and Radio stations
✅ An In-App Cinema Theater with smart IMDb resolvers & ad-blocking playback
✅ Powered by AI Voice Search (Whisper Large V3 Turbo + Deepgram Nova-2)
✅ Equipped with an audiophile-grade real-time 28-band FFT spectrum visualizer

Building this was easily one of the most rewarding engineering challenges of my career.

Some of the technical hurdles we conquered:
🔹 True Cross-Platform UI: Sharing >90% of declarative UI code between Android and Desktop using Compose Multiplatform while maintaining smooth 60fps animations and Material 3 adaptive themes.
🔹 Dual Native Media Engines: Powering desktop with low-latency libVLC C-bindings (via VLCJ) and Android with an AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer background foreground service.
🔹 Decoupled Plugin SPI: Creating a dynamic extension architecture where audio sources (Spotify, Saavn, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Genius Lyrics) act as modular, sandboxed plugins.
🔹 Seamless Video Streaming: Integrating an in-app Cinema studio that automatically queries Cinemeta IMDb metadata and renders video inside a popup-shielded sandbox.

The result? An ultra-smooth, lightweight app that plays anything you throw at it, anywhere you want it.

Huge shoutout to the Kotlin and Compose Multiplatform community for making modern cross-platform engineering such a joy.
